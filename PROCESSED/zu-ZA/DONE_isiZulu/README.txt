This dataset represents the text-to-speech resources that were developed under the
LWAZI III project by the Human Language Technologies Group of the Meraka 
Institute, CSIR.

The LWAZI III project was sponsored by the Department of Arts and
 Culture, the Government of the Republic of South Africa.

The resources consists of prompts and their associated recordings for the following
 languages:

Afrikaans,
 English, 
isiXhosa and 
isiZulu. 

Each language has it's own sub-directory where the resources are organised as follows:

-- utts.data
-- recordings/

The "utts.data" file contains the prompts developed under the project in the 
standard Festival utterance format, whilst the "recordings" folder contains the 
recordings conducted in a studio of the associated utterances. Note that there
are in some instances more utterances that actual recordings, this is due to
the time limitation of the recording sessions and the requirement of pre-compiling a
suitable list of utterances.
